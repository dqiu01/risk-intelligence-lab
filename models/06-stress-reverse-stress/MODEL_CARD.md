# Model Card — Stress & Reverse Stress Testing

**Purpose:** demonstrate deterministic stress design, nonlinear interaction losses, threshold analysis, and constrained reverse-stress optimization.

**Optimization:** minimize plausibility-weighted squared normalized shocks subject to modeled loss meeting/exceeding risk capacity and each shock staying inside its configured maximum.

**Limitations:** stress-factor loss functions are stylized, not empirically estimated; optimization identifies the nearest scenario under the chosen metric, not the objectively most likely crisis path.
