# Model Card — Monte Carlo Scenario Engine

**Purpose:** demonstrate uncertainty propagation, dependency, target-risk measurement, downside asymmetry, and sensitivity analysis.

**Assumptions:** normal latent driver shocks, user-specified volatilities and impacts, one shared systemic factor, optional common downside convexity.

**Limitations:** no empirical calibration in presets, no time-series dynamics, and no claim that driver correlation establishes causality.
