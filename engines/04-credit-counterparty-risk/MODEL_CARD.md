# Model Card — Credit / Counterparty Risk

**Purpose:** demonstrate expected loss, correlated default simulation, concentration analysis, tail-loss attribution, and credit stress testing.

**Key assumptions:** point-in-time PD inputs, EAD fixed over horizon, sector/global Gaussian dependence, beta-distributed LGD around a specified mean.

**Limitations:** no migration matrix, collateral waterfall, wrong-way risk engine, exposure amortization, or empirical calibration in synthetic presets.
